What home automation can we provide?

We implement solutions covering smart lighting control, appliance management, CCTV remote viewing, and scene-based scheduling (e.g. one button to turn off everything when leaving).

For new builds, wired automation offers maximum reliability. For existing homes, wireless smart switches provide practical automation without needing major rewiring.

What home automation can include

  • Smart lighting control (on/off, dimming, scheduling)
  • Scene-based control (e.g. "arrive home", "sleep")
  • Selected appliance on/off control
  • Remote monitoring and control via mobile
  • CCTV integration and remote viewing
  • Smart switches and panels
  • Automation scheduling
  • Expandable system design

Design considerations

What to consider when planning home automation

Infrastructure

Wired vs wireless automation

Wired systems are installed during construction for maximum reliability. Wireless systems replace existing switches, making them ideal for retrofitting existing homes.

Scope

Deciding what to automate

Not every appliance needs automation. We help you identify functions that truly add convenience, such as scheduled garden lighting or scene-based living room controls.

Network

Reliable internet and network

Remote monitoring via mobile apps requires a stable home Wi-Fi network. We can assess and upgrade your networking infrastructure if necessary.

Expansion

Future-ready system design

We design systems with expansion in mind, allowing you to add more smart devices or automate more rooms later without a complete redesign.

Common questions

Questions about home automation

Can automation be added to an existing house?

Yes, wireless smart switches replace your existing wall switches without major rewiring, connecting via your home's Wi-Fi network.

Can I control the system when away?

Yes. As long as your home and your phone both have internet access, you can monitor cameras and control lights via the mobile app.

Can automation integrate with CCTV?

Yes, camera feeds can often be viewed from the same mobile interface used to control your lights and appliances.

What happens during a power cut?

Most automated devices will stop working. However, critical monitoring systems like CCTV can be backed up with a UPS to stay online.
