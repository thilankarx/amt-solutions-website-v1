Design considerations

What affects CCTV system design?

A CCTV system that performs well in real conditions requires attention to several site-specific factors before cameras are placed or equipment is ordered.

Coverage

Camera placement and blind spots

We assess entry points, high-risk zones, and lighting conditions to determine the optimal camera quantity and mounting positions, ensuring no blind spots.

Lighting

Night visibility and low-light performance

Areas with limited lighting require cameras with infrared (IR) or low-light capability. We specify the correct IR range or supplementary lighting for optimal night vision.

Recording

Storage capacity and retention

Storage requirements depend on camera count, resolution, and retention days. We calculate the exact hard drive sizing during the design phase.

Network

Connectivity and remote access

IP systems require a robust data network. We assess your existing infrastructure to ensure seamless connectivity and stable remote mobile viewing.

Common questions

Questions about CCTV installation

How many cameras does a property need?

This depends on property size, required coverage zones, and budget. A site assessment is the most reliable way to determine the correct quantity.

Can I view CCTV cameras remotely on my phone?

Yes. All our modern systems support remote viewing via a mobile app, provided your site has a stable internet connection.

Can an existing CCTV system be upgraded?

Analog systems can often be upgraded to HD analog using existing cables. Moving to IP CCTV typically requires new cabling. We'll assess your setup to recommend the best path.

What is the difference between IP and HD analog?

IP cameras connect via network cables and support higher resolutions with PoE. HD analog cameras use coaxial cables and are great for upgrading existing setups without rewiring.

Does CCTV work during a power failure?

Only if you have a UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply). We can size and install a UPS to keep your cameras recording during outages.

Does CCTV require the internet to work?

No, local recording works without the internet. However, you do need an internet connection for remote mobile viewing.
