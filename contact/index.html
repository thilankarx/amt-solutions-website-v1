Helpful Information

What to include in your enquiry

Providing the following details helps our team prepare a practical assessment and accurate quotation for your property:

  • Site location: Specific suburb or area in Kandy or Sri Lanka
  • Property type: Residential home, office, retail shop, warehouse, hotel, school, or factory
  • Services required: CCTV installation, access control, fire alarms, networking, home automation, or PABX
  • System scope: Approximate camera count, entry doors, or area dimensions (if known)
  • Existing setup: Any current cabling or equipment already in place
  • Preferred timing: Suitable date/time for an on-site evaluation
