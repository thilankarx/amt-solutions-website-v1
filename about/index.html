About AMT Solutions

Security and smart technology integration since 2014

AMT Solutions is a Sri Lanka-based technology integrator specialising in CCTV, access control, fire alarms, networking, home automation, and PABX systems.

What AMT Solutions does

We design and install security, connectivity, and smart technology systems tailored for homes, offices, hotels, retail spaces, schools, and factories across Sri Lanka.

Rather than pushing standard product packages, we evaluate each site individually. This approach ensures all equipment, cabling, and network integrations are practical and highly stable.

Services provided

  • CCTV & Surveillance Systems
  • Access Control Systems
  • Fire Alarm Systems
  • Home Automation
  • Networking & Structured Cabling
  • PABX & Communication Systems

Our approach

How AMT Solutions approaches each project

Every security and smart technology project follows a consistent process to ensure the final system meets the site-specific requirements of the client.

01

Site Assessment

We visit the site to understand property layout, coverage areas, existing infrastructure, and specific security requirements.

02

Solution Design

We specify a practical architecture, selecting appropriate equipment, cabling, and network integration tailored to your needs.

03

Professional Installation

Complete installation, equipment mounting, network setup, and rigorous testing before client handover.

04

Handover & Support

We provide a complete user handover covering system operation and offer ongoing support for maintenance and future expansions.
